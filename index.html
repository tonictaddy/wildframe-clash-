YOUR HAND
Select a card, then a location to play it.
Wildframe: Clash begins. Win at least two locations after round six.
Tap any friendly deployed card to inspect and activate its ability. Cooldowns are counted in rounds and each character can activate at most once per round. Team effects trigger automatically when their required cards are on the board. Prototype rules: Each round you gain energy equal to the round number. Unspent energy does not carry forward. Select a card in your hand, then tap a location. Each location holds up to four cards per side. Cards have Cost (blue) and Power (gold). The opponent acts when you end the round. Tied locations count for neither player. Abilities and locations are prototype designs subject to change.
